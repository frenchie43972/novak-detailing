What I do

Detailing services

Every job is quoted for the vehicle in front of me. No rushed, one-size-fits-all packages.

Interior detailing

Seats, carpets, mats, dash, console, vents, door panels and glass, cleaned carefully down to the cupholders and seat tracks.

Exterior detailing

A thorough hand wash and decontamination, plus wheels, tires and wheel wells, finished with clean glass and dressed trim.

Buffing & polishing

Paint correction that lifts swirl marks, haze and light scratches to bring back depth and gloss.

Ceramic coatings

Long-lasting protection that makes paint easier to keep clean and keeps it looking freshly detailed for longer.

Pet hair removal

Embedded dog and cat hair pulled out of carpet, cargo areas and upholstery.

Sticker & decal removal

Stickers, decals and adhesive removed cleanly without damaging the paint or glass underneath.

Do you come to me, or do I bring the car to you?

Either one. I can detail your vehicle at your location with mobile equipment, or pick it up, detail it at the warehouse and return it to you.

How much does a detail cost?

Interior or exterior details are $150–$200, and a full interior and exterior detail is $275–$375. Where it lands in that range depends on the vehicle's size and condition. Buffing, ceramic coatings, pet hair removal and decal removal are quoted separately.

How do I get an exact quote?

Text 740-827-8386 with your vehicle's year, make and model, the service you want, your town, and a few photos of the inside and outside. You'll get a firm price before anything is scheduled.

When are appointments available?

Weekdays after 4:30 p.m., and any time on weekends.

What areas do you serve?

Akron, Canton and the surrounding Northeast Ohio communities. Not sure if you're in range? Text your town and I'll let you know.
